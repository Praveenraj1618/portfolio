## Selected work

### Legal AI Intelligence & Negotiation Platform
Built an evidence-grounded legal-document platform with hybrid retrieval, Reciprocal Rank Fusion, reranking, clause-level citations, document analysis, and grounded question answering. A controlled one-contract benchmark compared five retrieval configurations and recorded stronger top-result retrieval after reranking.

**Technologies:** Python, LangChain, ChromaDB, BM25, dense embeddings, reranking, FastAPI, React

### Virtual Try-On
Developing a person-based virtual try-on workflow that combines garment masking, CatVTON inference, compositing diagnostics, and a separate procedural 3D fitting experience. The project focuses on garment variety, identity preservation, and practical quality controls.

**Technologies:** PyTorch, CatVTON, FastAPI, React, Three.js

### AgroVision — Multi-Crop Disease Detection
Designed a two-stage computer-vision pipeline that uses YOLO to locate candidate regions and EfficientNet-B0 to classify crop conditions across five crop families and fifteen labels.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, YOLOv8, EfficientNet-B0, OpenCV, ONNX, FastAPI

### Company Lens
Built a multilingual company-monitoring workspace that collects updates from regional and global sources, groups related reporting, preserves citations, exposes source health, and supports retrieval-based analysis.

**Technologies:** TypeScript, React, Cloudflare, BM25, data pipelines

### Integrated Track Monitoring System
Designed a railway-track inspection prototype using YOLOv8 for defect localization and classification, paired with a review dashboard for annotated detections and location-aware maintenance workflows.

**Technologies:** Python, YOLOv8, OpenCV, React

### Speech to Sign
Created an accessible communication prototype that accepts English, Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi speech, converts recognized text into simplified gloss, and maps words to sign clips with fingerspelling fallback.

**Technologies:** Python, spaCy, SpeechRecognition, Streamlit, OpenCV

### AI & Workflow Automation
During my internship at Marobust Tech and Solutions, I delivered n8n workflows across client projects and contributed to a hospital appointment platform with real-time slot availability, administrative controls, and Telegram-based patient interactions.

**Technologies:** n8n, Telegram bots, third-party integrations, workflow automation, web application development

### WasteZero
Built a full-stack community coordination prototype with role-based access, opportunity and pickup workflows, database-backed messaging, and real-time Socket.IO updates.

**Technologies:** React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Socket.IO, JWT
